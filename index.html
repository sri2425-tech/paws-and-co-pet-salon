Frequently asked questions

A few common questions about grooming, appointments and caring for your pet.

What happens during a grooming appointment?

We start by understanding your pet’s coat and temperament, then move through bathing, drying, brushing, grooming and finishing. We keep the process gentle and give your pet breaks when needed.

How long does grooming take?

It depends on your pet’s size, coat, grooming needs and temperament. Most appointments take around 1.5–3 hours, and we’ll give you a more accurate estimate when you book.

Do you groom both dogs and cats?

Yes. We offer grooming and hygiene services for dogs and cats, with the service adapted to each pet’s needs.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking button or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. Share your pet’s breed, size and grooming needs, and we’ll help you choose the right service.
